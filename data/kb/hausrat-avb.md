## Insured perils
Hausrat Comfort covers household contents against fire, lightning, explosion, burglary and robbery, vandalism after a break-in, water damage (escape of tap water), and storm or hail. Natural hazards such as flooding, heavy-rain flooding, backwater, earthquake, landslide and snow load are not covered unless the policy includes the natural hazards add-on (Elementarschaden-Baustein).

## Water damage (escape of tap water)
Covered: water escaping unintentionally from pipes, connected appliances such as washing machines and dishwashers, and heating systems, including damage caused by burst pipes and frost damage to pipes. Not covered: water from rainwater downpipes, gradual seepage over a long period, groundwater, and flooding. Frost damage in a building that is not permanently inhabited is covered only if the policyholder heated the building adequately or drained and shut off all water pipes.

## Burglary
Burglary cover applies when a burglar enters the home by breaking in, climbing in, or using false keys. The policyholder must report the burglary to the police without undue delay and give both the police and Contoso a list of the stolen items. Without a police report reference number the claim cannot be settled.

## Storm and hail
Storm means air movement of at least wind force 8 on the Beaufort scale. If the wind force at the location cannot be determined, storm is assumed when the wind has also damaged properly maintained buildings in the vicinity. Damage at wind force 7 or below is not storm damage.

## Underinsurance waiver
Contoso waives the underinsurance deduction if the sum insured is at least 650 EUR per square metre of living area at the time of the loss. Otherwise the payout is reduced in the proportion of the sum insured to the actual value of the contents.

## Obligations after a loss
The policyholder must minimise the loss, report it without undue delay, allow Contoso to inspect the damage, and keep damaged items until the inspection, unless Contoso agrees otherwise.
