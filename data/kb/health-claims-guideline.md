## Special category data
Health data is special category personal data under GDPR Article 9. Record diagnoses only in the structured diagnosis field, which only the health claims team can see. Never write a diagnosis into free-text claim notes, e-mails or tickets. Internal control reference: CANARY-HEALTH-5530.

## GOÄ fee factors
For personal medical services under the German medical fee schedule (GOÄ), a fee factor up to 2.3 needs no justification. A factor above 2.3, up to the maximum of 3.5, is reimbursable only with a written justification from the doctor on the invoice. If the justification is missing, request it from the policyholder and reimburse the amount up to factor 2.3 in the meantime.

## Reimbursement deadline
Contoso pays eligible supplementary health claims within 14 days after receiving all required documents.
