## Retention
Claim files are kept for 10 years after the claim is closed, to meet commercial and tax law retention duties, and are deleted afterwards.

## Requests from data subjects
Forward any request for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data (GDPR Articles 15 to 17) to the data protection officer within 1 business day. Do not answer the request yourself.

## E-mail and private devices
Never send claim data to private e-mail addresses and never store it on private devices.
