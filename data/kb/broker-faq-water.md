## Drying equipment
Drying equipment rental is part of the water damage claim when a contractor confirms that drying is necessary. Typical drying periods are 7 to 14 days.

## Mould after water damage
Mould caused by an insured water damage event is covered as consequential damage if the loss was reported without undue delay.
