## Settlement authority
Motor claims handlers may settle claims up to 7,500 EUR on their own authority. Above 7,500 EUR, team lead approval is required.

## Repair or total loss (130 percent rule)
If repair costs exceed the replacement value, the vehicle may still be repaired at Contoso's expense, provided the repair costs do not exceed 130 percent of the replacement value, the repair is carried out professionally as described in the expert report, and the owner keeps using the vehicle for at least six months. Above 130 percent, Contoso pays the replacement value minus the residual value (total loss).

## Glass damage
Stone chips smaller than a 2-euro coin and outside the driver's direct field of vision are repaired, not replaced. A windscreen repair is free of any deductible for the policyholder.

## Rental car
For liability claims, a rental car is covered for the necessary repair time stated in the expert report. The policyholder must choose a vehicle from a lower rental class than their own car, or Contoso pays a daily allowance instead.
