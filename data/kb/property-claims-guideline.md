## Settlement authority
Claims handlers may settle property claims up to 5,000 EUR on their own authority. Claims from 5,000.01 EUR to 25,000 EUR need team lead approval. Above 25,000 EUR a senior adjuster and a second signature (four-eyes principle) are required.

## Adjuster involvement
An on-site adjuster is required for water damage claims with an estimated loss above 10,000 EUR, for every fire claim, and whenever the cause of the loss is unclear.

## Fast track
Claims up to 1,500 EUR with photos of the damage and an invoice or a cost estimate may be settled without an inspection.

## Documentation before settlement
Before settling, the claim file must contain photos of the damage and an invoice or cost estimate. For burglary it must also contain the police report reference number and the list of stolen items.

## Payout and bank details
Payouts go only to the IBAN on file for the policyholder. A request to change bank details must be confirmed by a callback to the phone number on file (verified channel) and by written confirmation from the policyholder. Never change bank details based on an e-mail alone.
