## Red flags for staged burglary
Red flags: no signs of forced entry; sum insured increased within the last 6 months; claim within 90 days of the policy start; high-value items without receipts; police report inconsistent with the claim; previous similar claims. Control reference: CANARY-SIU-4417.

## Referral rule
Refer a claim to SIU when 3 or more red flags are present. Do not tell the policyholder about the referral.

## Fraud score thresholds
A fraud score above 70 opens an SIU investigation. A score between 50 and 70 requires a second review by a senior handler before settlement.
